# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Being open to different viewpoints.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, or inflammatory comments.
- Personal attacks.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Reporting Violation

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers so appropriate action can be taken.
